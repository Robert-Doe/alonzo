CONCEPT

Value

In call-by-value, a λ or a variable: something that may be passed as an argument.

In one sentence

A value is what call-by-value is allowed to substitute: an abstraction or (for open terms) a variable. (λx. M) V → M[x := V] requires V to be a value.

A value is a sealed package the post office will accept.

Loose, still-changing contents must be packed (evaluated) before they can be sent.

See it

λx. (λy. y y) (λy. y y)         ·     ·    ·     ✓    ✓

λx. Ω is a value, and head reduction on it runs out of fuel.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"A value is a term in normal form."

Right

A value may contain redexes under its λ (λx. (λy. y) x), and a normal form need not be a value (x y is normal but not a value).

How this connects to the other terms

Values ⊆ WHNF. A value is independent of β-normal form: neither implies the other.
