CONCEPT

β-normal form

No β-redex anywhere, including under λ. What normal order produces.

In one sentence

A term is in β-normal form when no sub-term has the shape (λx. M) N. It's what "the normal form" means by default.

Every envelope in the pile has been opened, even the ones tucked inside folders.

Whatever is left can't compute any further.

See it

x ((λa. a) b)                   ·     ·    ✓     ✓    ·

Head normal, but not β-normal: a redex remains inside the argument.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"If a term's head is a variable, it's in normal form."

Right

That's head normal form. β-normal form also requires that the arguments (and everything under every λ) be redex-free.

How this connects to the other terms

Stricter: βη-normal form. Looser: head normal form. Unique when it exists, by Church–Rosser (Module 10).
