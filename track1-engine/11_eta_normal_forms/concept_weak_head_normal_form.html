CONCEPT

Weak head normal form

A λ, or a variable applied to arguments. Where lazy evaluation stops.

In one sentence

A term is in weak head normal form (WHNF) when it is an abstraction λx. M (anything inside) or a neutral term y M1 … Mk.

A wrapped present: you know it's a present, and you haven't opened it.

Lazy languages stop here until someone actually needs what's inside.

See it

to WHNF (call-by-name)             λy. (λz. (λw. w) z) y        1 step(s)
to HNF (head reduction)            λy. y                        3 step(s)

Real demo output: call-by-name stops as soon as the term is a λ.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"A term in WHNF has finished computing."

Right

WHNF only fixes the outer shape. λx. Ω is in WHNF, yet running its body never finishes. Haskell's seq evaluates to exactly this form.

How this connects to the other terms

The loosest form in the chain: HNF ⊆ WHNF. Every value is in WHNF.
