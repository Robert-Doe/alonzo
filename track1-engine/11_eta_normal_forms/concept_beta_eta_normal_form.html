CONCEPT

βη-normal form

No β-redex and no η-redex anywhere. The strictest notion of finished.

In one sentence

A term is in βη-normal form when it contains neither (λx. M) N nor λx. M x (x ∉ FV(M)) anywhere, even under λ.

A fully simplified fraction: nothing cancels and nothing can be rewritten shorter.

Not even a forwarding wrapper is left.

See it

f                               ✓     ✓    ✓     ✓    ✓
λx. f x                         ·     ✓    ✓     ✓    ✓

Real demo output (columns: βη-NF, β-NF, HNF, WHNF, value). λx. f x is β-normal but not βη-normal: it's an η-redex.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"β-normal and βη-normal are the same thing."

Right

They differ exactly by η. λx. f x is β-normal, and η-reduces to f. βη-NF is where two extensionally equal functions become syntactically equal.

How this connects to the other terms

Every βη-normal form is a β-normal form, which is a head normal form, which is a WHNF.
