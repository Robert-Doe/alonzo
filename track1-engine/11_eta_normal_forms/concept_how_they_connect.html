VOCABULARY HUB

βη-NF · β-NF · HNF · WHNF · Value

Five meanings of "finished". They nest inside each other, and each has a term that sits in it but not in the next.

Definitions, side by side

βη-normal form
No β-redex and no η-redex anywhere. The strictest notion of finished.
β-normal form
No β-redex anywhere, including under λ. What normal order produces.
Head normal form
λx1…xn. y M1 … Mk: a variable sits at the head. Arguments may still compute.
Weak head normal form
A λ, or a variable applied to arguments. Where lazy evaluation stops.
Value
In call-by-value, a λ or a variable: something that may be passed as an argument.

One picture: where each term lives

Nested: βη-NF ⊆ β-NF ⊆ HNF ⊆ WHNF (each example sits in its box but not the next inner one). Values (dashed) lie inside WHNF and overlap the inner boxes: f and λx. f x are values, x ((λa. a) b) is not.

Commonly confused pairs

PairThe wrong statementThe correction
β-NF vs βη-NF"λx. f x is fully simplified."It's β-normal but η-reduces to f.
HNF vs WHNF"λx. (λy. y) x is in head normal form: it starts with λ."Starting with λ gives WHNF. HNF looks under the λ, where the head is a redex.
WHNF vs finished"It's in WHNF, so evaluation is done."Only the outer shape is fixed. λx. Ω is WHNF with an infinite body.
value vs normal form"Values are normal forms."λx. (λy. y) x is a value, not normal. x y is normal, not a value.
HNF vs β-NF"Head variable means done."Arguments may still hold redexes: x ((λa. a) b).
