CONCEPT

Head normal form

λx1…xn. y M1 … Mk: a variable sits at the head. Arguments may still compute.

In one sentence

A term is in head normal form (HNF) when, after its leading λs, its head is a variable. Nothing can ever change that outline, whatever happens in the arguments.

The headline of a newspaper is printed. The articles below may still be edited.

Whatever edits happen, the front page's headline is final.

See it

x ((λa. a) b)                   ·     ·    ✓     ✓    ·
λx. (λy. y) x                   ·     ·    ·     ✓    ✓

The first is HNF (head x). The second is not: under its λ, the head is a redex.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"HNF and WHNF differ only in the word 'weak'."

Right

HNF looks under the leading λs, and WHNF doesn't. λx. (λy. y) x is WHNF (it starts with λ) but not HNF (under that λ the head is a redex).

How this connects to the other terms

Between β-normal form (stricter) and WHNF (looser). Terms with no HNF (like λx. Ω) are called unsolvable, the λ-calculus's notion of "meaningless".
