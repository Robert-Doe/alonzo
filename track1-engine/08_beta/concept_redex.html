CONCEPT

Redex

The sub-term about to be rewritten: a rule's left side, found somewhere inside a term.

In one sentence

A redex is a sub-term matching a rule's left side: (λx. M) N for β, or λx. M x (x ∉ FV(M)) for η. It's the before of one step.

The redex is the sealed envelope you're about to open.

It's still in the pile, exactly as written. Nothing has happened to it yet.

See it

(λx. x x) ([(λy. y) z])
  context    C[□] = (λx. x x) □
  redex           = (λy. y) z
  contractum      = z
  reduct  C[…]    = (λx. x x) z

Real npm run demo output for one step.

Common mix-up

Wrong

"The redex is the whole term being reduced."

Right

The redex is the one sub-term that gets rewritten. The whole term is context + redex. Here, the whole term is (λx. x x) ((λy. y) z) and the redex is only (λy. y) z.

How this connects to the other terms

A redex sits inside a context. Contracting it gives the contractum. Putting that back into the context gives the reduct.
